Privacy Policy

Clipboard Translator Subtitles (Microsoft Edge extension)
Last updated: 2026-08-13

Summary

This extension helps you look up English words on ordinary web pages and look up or translate text from YouTube subtitles. It works together with the optional open-source desktop app Clipboard Translator installed on the same computer. The extension does not send your data to servers operated by the extension developer for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

Data the extension accesses

  • Website content: on ordinary pages, the extension acts only after you double-click a single English word and reads that word plus its containing sentence (limited to 500 characters). On YouTube, it reads the active subtitle line and the word or phrase you explicitly click or select. Editable fields are excluded from web lookup.
  • Local settings: preference values such as feature on/off, target language, local bridge port, and a pairing token are stored with chrome.storage.local in your browser profile.

How that data is used

The selected word and sentence or selected subtitle text is sent first to the Clipboard Translator desktop app on your machine (loopback HTTP to 127.0.0.1, and optionally native messaging to obtain local bridge credentials). The desktop app performs lookup or translation using the API endpoint and API key that you configure. The extension package itself does not call cloud AI APIs and does not upload page text to the developer. The desktop app may query FreeDictionaryAPI, Wikimedia/Wiktionary, and, only when you configure a key, Merriam-Webster to obtain dictionary facts.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, address, age, or other contact details
  • Health, financial, or payment data
  • Passwords or account credentials for websites, YouTube, Google, or Microsoft
  • Emails, chats, or personal communications
  • Precise location or GPS
  • Full browsing history for advertising or analytics
  • Keystroke logging or full-page/background reading unrelated to an explicit lookup

Sharing and selling

We do not sell user data. We do not transfer subtitle text or settings to third parties for advertising, credit scoring, or unrelated purposes. If you configure a third-party AI API in the desktop app, that provider’s own privacy policy applies to the requests you choose to send there.
